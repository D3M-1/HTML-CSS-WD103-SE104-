📱 Device Guide & Offline Operation

This application is a standalone, offline digital portal covering all 5 core lectures and applied engineering labs of SE104 Human-Computer Interaction & Interface Design under Dr. Reda Zayed.

1. Apple iPad & iPhone (Safari):

  • Tap the Share button and choose "Add to Home Screen".
  • Launches in full-screen standalone mode with no URL bars and instant offline loading.

2. Android Phones & Tablets (Chrome):

  • Tap the menu (three dots) and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

3. Direct PDF Printing:

  • Click "Blank Exam (PDF)" to print or save a clean blank exam paper with ruled dotted lines.
  • Click "Answer Key (PDF)" to print the complete model answers with explanations.
